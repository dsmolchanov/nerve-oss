# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and clear when contributing to Nerve. Welcome people
with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss technical choices
with evidence; critique work without attacking the person who produced it.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.
Do not use project issues or pull requests to target or repeatedly disrupt others.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed
community spaces, including interactions where someone represents the project.

Report concerns privately to **support@nerve.email**. Describe what happened and
include relevant links where available. Share only information needed to assess
the concern; do not post private reports publicly.

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, remove inappropriate
content, restrict participation or ban repeated/severe violations. Responses should
be proportionate and protect affected participants. Reports will be handled with
care; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where action requires sharing details.
If you disagree with a moderation decision, contact the same address with context.
